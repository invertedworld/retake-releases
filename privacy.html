Retake — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

What Retake collects

Nothing. Retake has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. It does not collect, store or send personal data, usage data or diagnostics.

What stays on your Mac

Everything you record or make with Retake — screen recordings, camera and microphone recordings, narration, captions, projects and exported videos — is written to the folders you choose on your Mac and goes nowhere else unless you share it yourself.

Transcription

Captions are transcribed from your narration on your Mac using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The first time, macOS may download its speech model from Apple; that download is a macOS service and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Your audio is not sent anywhere.

Permissions

Retake asks macOS for Screen Recording access to record the screen, and for Microphone, Camera, Input Monitoring (for the keystroke overlay) and Speech Recognition (for captions) only when you turn those features on. You can change each of them at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Purchases

On the Mac App Store, the unlock is handled entirely by Apple. Retake receives only Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made; it never sees your payment details or Apple Account.

Updates

Retake from the Mac App Store is updated by the App Store. Retake downloaded from this site checks this site for a new version about once a day; the request carries no personal information.
